I'm not a Fernandes customer. Can I still use the 24/7 service?

The 24/7 service is only for vehicles bought at Fernandes Autohandel. Flat battery with another car? Call our Parts department on 475046 ext. 2306/2308, or WhatsApp 898-8144.

How do I pay for the 24/7 service?

Pay within three working days at Fernandes Autohandel, Keizerstraat 105-117.

Does it cover an accident?

Collisions aren't covered by the 24/7 service. We can help you arrange a tow truck, so your vehicle gets to our workshop safely.

In which districts can I call on you?

Paramaribo, Wanica, Commewijne and Saramacca.

What if it can't be fixed on the spot?

We help you contact a towing service so your vehicle is brought safely to our workshop.
